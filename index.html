Notchwave Privacy Policy

App: Notchwave for WindowsDeveloper: Som Anand SahooEffective date: 7 November 2026
Contact: somanandsahoo96@gmail.com

Notchwave is a Windows app that shows music, notifications, timers and other information in a small notch at the top of your screen. This policy explains what Notchwave accesses, where that data goes, how long it is kept and how you can delete it.

In short

  • No account is required. There is no sign-in, and Notchwave has no servers of its own.
  • No ads, no analytics, no telemetry. The developer does not collect, receive or store any of your data.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone for advertising or any other purpose.
  • Almost everything happens only on your PC. Notchwave goes online only for features you set up yourself (weather, your calendar link, synced lyrics), each described below.

All of these files are kept on your PC in Notchwave's own folder, %LOCALAPPDATA%\Notchwave. For the Microsoft Store version, Windows keeps that folder inside the app's private data, and uninstalling Notchwave deletes it. The logs contain technical errors only. They never contain notification text, clipboard contents, notes, file names or anything you typed.

Internet connections

Each connection goes directly from your PC to the service named. The developer never sees it. As with any internet request, the service sees your IP address and the app's name and version.

The Microsoft Store version never checks for updates itself: the Store updates it. (Versions downloaded from GitHub can check the public release list on GitHub once a day, only if you enter a repository in Settings.)

Screen sharing

Screen sharing starts only when you start it. Viewers on your own local network scan a QR code and connect directly to your PC, and you approve each viewer. The picture goes over a direct, peer-to-peer connection that stays on your local network: no relay or cloud server, and nothing passes through the developer. It is off on networks Windows marks as Public, and it stops by itself after a time limit you set. The sharing page runs in Microsoft Edge WebView2, a Windows component loaded only while you share; Microsoft's own terms apply to it.

Sharing with third parties

Notchwave does not sell, rent or share your personal data. The only data that reaches a third party is what you ask for through the internet features above.

Children

Notchwave is a general-purpose utility. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and it has no accounts, chat or ads.

Your choices

  • Turn any feature off in Settings → Features.
  • Withdraw notification, camera, microphone or Bluetooth access at any time in Windows Settings → Privacy & security.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling Notchwave. For versions not from the Store, also delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\Notchwave (and %APPDATA%\DynamicIsland if an older version left it).
